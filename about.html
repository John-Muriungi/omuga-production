About Me

Creative visionary with a passion for visual storytelling

The Journey

I've been transforming ideas into compelling visual narratives that resonate with audiences and elevate brands.

My journey began with a simple camera and a passion for capturing moments. What started as a personal interest evolved into a professional career spanning commercial photography, cinematic videography, and creative direction for global brands.

I believe that every project tells a story, and my role is to ensure that story is told with authenticity, creativity, and technical excellence. Whether working with Fortune 500 companies or passionate startups, I bring the same dedication to visual storytelling.

Based in Kenya, working countrywide.
